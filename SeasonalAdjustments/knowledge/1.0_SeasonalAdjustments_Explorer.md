# 1.0 Seasonal Adjustments Explorer

## Purpose
A standalone educational app that walks a reader — starting from zero — through the mechanics of TIPS seasonal adjustment: the raw CPI seasonal cycle → the SA Factor derived from it → how that factor shifts TIPS prices and yields. Presented as a paged "graphic novel": one big visual per page, minimal caption text, a nested table of contents for jumping to any topic, and Prev/Next for linear reading. This replaced an earlier flat 3-tab layout — see "Book Structure" below.

## Dependencies
- [YieldCurves/knowledge/1.0_Seasonal_Adjustments.md](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/1.0_Seasonal_Adjustments.md) — canonical SA math (P_SA = P × S(settle)/S(maturity))
- [YieldCurves/knowledge/Canty.md](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/Canty.md) — canonical derivation (Eq. 1–21) behind Chapter 3's cash-flow/seasonal-gap picture

## Book Structure (Chapters & Pages)

Content is organized as chapters, each with one or more pages, flattened into a single ordered `PAGES` array in the script (index = reading order). Navigation:
- **Topbar**: portal link, `☰ Contents` toggle, book title, breadcrumb (`Chapter · page N/total`)
- **TOC drawer** (`#toc`): nested chapter → page list, hardcoded in HTML (not generated), each `<li data-page="N">` wired to `showPage(N)`. Opens via the topbar button, closes on scrim click, Escape, or page navigation.
- **Stage** (`#stage`): one `.page` div per page, all present in the DOM; `showPage(n)` toggles the `.on` class (only the current page is visible) — same visibility mechanism the old tab bar used (`switchTab`), just renamed and driven by an index instead of 3 fixed buttons.
- **Pager** (`#pagerbar`): Prev/Next buttons (disabled at the first/last page — no wraparound) + dot progress indicator. Left/Right arrow keys also navigate (ignored while the TOC is open).

### Adding a new page
1. Add a `.page` div to `#stage` with a unique id.
2. Add a matching entry to the `PAGES` array (chapter label + title) at the same index.
3. Add a `<li data-page="N">` under the right chapter in `#toc`'s `.toc-pages` list.
4. If the page needs a chart, give its `<svg>` a unique id and write/reuse a `drawX()` function that targets it.

## Data Source

**R2 file**: `TIPS/RefCpiNsaSa.csv`
Columns: `date, nsa, sa` (daily Ref CPI NSA and SA, produced by `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` App. B interpolation).
The SA Factor for any date is computed on the fly: `factor = nsa / sa`.

**10-year window** (Chapter 1, both pages): rows from `${maxYear−11}-01-01` through the latest date in the file. This gives ≥10 full calendar years of month-over-month data. Chapter 1's charts have **no offline fallback** (unlike `daily`, below) — if the R2 fetch fails, both pages render blank except title/caption/legend.

**5-year average** (Chapter 2, Chapter 3): for each calendar day `mm-dd`, average the SA Factor across the 5 most recent complete years. Used for the annual curve and the interactive price-adjustment slider. Falls back to `buildDailyFromMonthly()` (monthly anchors interpolated to daily) if the R2 fetch fails.

## Chapter 3 — Settlement, Cash Flows & Maturity

### Concept
Price is the sum of present-valued nominal cash flows — coupons and, in the final period, principal (see [Canty §3](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/Canty.md#3-derivation-of-the-seasonally-adjusted-clean-price-annual-coupons)). The SA transform multiplies the whole **clean price** by one ratio, `S(settle)/S(maturity)`, using only the seasonal factor at the *final* payment date (Canty's paper calls this "Eq. 14" — the app avoids that label in user-facing text and just describes the formula). That's a good approximation (see [Canty §1a](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/Canty.md#1a-tips-translation-cantys-notation--us-tips-terms) and [2.2 §3.3](../../YieldCurves/knowledge/2.2_SAO_Residual_Analysis.md)) specifically because for real (semiannual) TIPS the correction from the *other* coupon month is ≤1bp — it rides on the small coupon stream, while the dominant PV (the principal) sits in the maturity month exactly. This chapter goes one step further and **assumes annual coupons** (Canty's own base-case simplification, Eq. 1–14), so every payment date — not just the principal — shares the maturity month/day exactly, and every seasonal-gap bar in the chart is visually identical. The seasonal gap itself (`S(settle)` vs. `S(paydate)`) is not unique to maturity — it recurs, at the same magnitude, at every payment date, because S is periodic. What makes maturity special isn't a bigger gap; it's that maturity is the one payment date carrying the principal. The chart makes both halves visible at once: the periodic gap (via the wave/reference-line geometry that worked in the original single-year chart) and the cash-flow weighting (via bar thickness), rather than folding the wave into one year or switching to a polar/PV-dot chart.

A second, distinct point the years-to-maturity control demonstrates: the **price**-adjustment ratio is maturity-independent (same mm/dd gap every year), but the **yield** impact of that same price gap shrinks as maturity/duration grows, because a fixed price move is a smaller annualized yield move when spread over more years (`Δy ≈ -%ΔPrice / ModDuration`). The sidebar's "Approx. SA yield impact" stat makes this explicit and is the reason the years-to-maturity buttons exist at all.

### Illustrative bond
Not a live calculator — a fixed stand-in so the picture doesn't need real trade data:
- **Coupon**: 1.25% (`ILLUS_COUPON`), the average coupon across all outstanding TIPS (`TipsRef.csv`, checked 2026-07-11), paid **annually** (simplified from TIPS' real semiannual schedule — see Concept above)
- **Yield**: `ILLUS_YIELD_CURVE`, a small lookup of approximate real yield by years-to-maturity (1/2/3/5 → ~2.07%/1.88%/1.86%/1.9%), from a FedInvest snapshot (2026-07-10), linearly interpolated. Capped at 5y on purpose — the seasonal effect is only visible/relevant in the short end; 10/20/30y added nothing to this picture and were dropped.
- **Cash-flow schedule**: `cashFlowSchedule()` / `finalElapsed()` — the final payment is pinned to the true anniversary of the selected maturity month/day closest to the selected years-to-maturity (so the schedule stays mm/dd-consistent, at the cost of the actual horizon sometimes differing slightly from the button label — the readout panel's "Illustrative bond" line shows the real value). Coupons step back one full year at a time. PV via annual discounting (`pv = cf/(1+yld)^yearsElapsed`). Deliberately simplified, not a duplicate of the real semiannual Actual/Actual pricing in `shared/src/bond-math.js`, which this app does not need.
- **Modified duration**: `modifiedDuration()` — Macaulay duration computed directly from the schedule's own PV-weighted years (`Σ(yearsElapsed·pv)/Σpv`), divided by `(1+yld)` for annual compounding. A local calc consistent with the annual-coupon simplification, not a call into `bond-math.js`'s semiannual `calculateMDuration`.

### Seasonal-gap timeline chart
Cartesian, restoring the original chart's visual grammar instead of a spiral or PV-sized dots:
- X = days elapsed since settlement, 0 → the final payment (not calendar day-of-year folded into one year). The S(t) wave is plotted across this whole span, so the same annual cycle from Chapter 2 visibly repeats once per year of the bond's life.
- A horizontal dashed reference line marks **S(settle)**, fixed once you buy.
- Every payment date gets a **vertical bar** from that reference line to the wave — this bar height *is* `S(settle)` vs. `S(paydate)`, the same quantity the price-adjustment ratio applies to price. Under the annual-coupon simplification every bar lands on the same point of the wave, so every bar is the same height and color.
- Bar **color**: green (`--up`) when `S(paydate) > S(settle)` (SA yield rises), red (`--down`) when `S(paydate) < S(settle)` (SA yield falls) — same convention as the original bracket.
- Bar **thickness** ∝ that payment's present value, **linearly**: `w = 1.2 + 22·(pv/maxPv)`, min ~1.2px, max ~23.2px. (Was `11·√(pv/maxPv)` — sqrt scaling compresses the visual contrast, which reads as "not proportional"; switched to linear per explicit feedback, with a larger max width for a more dramatic thin-coupons/thick-principal contrast.) This is the price = Σ PV(cash flows) weighting layered onto the periodic gap.
- The final bar gets a `%` label (`(S(settle)/S(final)−1)×100`, matching the sidebar's price-adjustment ratio) and a maturity dot.
- Year-boundary gridlines (`+1y`, `+2y`, …) and month ticks (major Jan, minor Apr/Jul/Oct) run the length of the chart for calendar orientation.

### Controls
- **Settlement date slider** — defaults to the next bond trading day after today (T+1, skipping weekends and SIFMA holidays via `defaultSettleDoy()` / `shared/src/settlement.js`'s `nextBusinessDay`), not a fixed placeholder date. Holiday fetch (`misc/BondHolidaysSifma.csv`) is optional with a 3s timeout; falls back to weekend-only skip.
- Maturity month/day buttons (drives `matIdx`)
- **Years-to-maturity buttons**: 1 / 2 / 3 / 5, drives `yearsToMat` — sets both the chart's time span and the cash-flow schedule length

### Readout panel
- S(settle), S(final payment), first-coupon stub length, price-adjustment ratio S(settle)/S(final), "capture" S(final)/S(settle) − 1, verdict text (explains the gap recurs annually and only the last occurrence carries principal)
- **Approx. SA yield impact** (bps) — the price gap converted to a yield effect via modified duration; this is the number that visibly shrinks as the years-to-maturity buttons increase, demonstrating the actual point of that control
- **Illustrative bond** line: the *actual* modeled horizon/coupon/yield (may differ slightly from the button's round number — see schedule note above)
- **Principal share of price** vs **all coupons combined** (with payment count) — the PV split backing the bar-thickness encoding

### Sat() rounding
`Sat(d)` rounds `d` to the nearest integer day before indexing into the 365-element `daily` array. Needed because `cashFlowSchedule()` (in an earlier semiannual version) produced half-day elapsed values; a fractional array index silently returns `undefined`. Kept as a defensive rounding even after the switch to annual (whole-day) steps.

## Key Formulas

| Symbol | Definition |
|--------|-----------|
| S(t) | Ref CPI NSA(t) / Ref CPI SA(t) — SA Factor on day t |
| P_SA | P_market × S(settle) / S(maturity) — seasonally adjusted price |
| Ratio | S(settle) / S(maturity) — the price multiplier |
| Capture | S(maturity) / S(settle) − 1 — % impact on price |

## Architecture
- Single file: `SeasonalAdjustments/index.html` — no build step, `<script type="module">` (imports `shared/src/settlement.js`, `shared/src/csv.js`)
- Data fetched from R2 at load; 6-second timeout; falls back to `buildDailyFromMonthly()` (monthly anchors interpolated to daily) if fetch fails — but only for `daily` (Chapter 2/3); Chapter 1's `series`-based charts have no fallback (see Data Source)
- `drawCpiChart()` — Chapter 1, Page 1
- `drawFactorChart()` — Chapter 1, Page 2
- `drawAnnualCurve()` — Chapter 2
- `defaultSettleDoy()` — Chapter 3, T+1 bond-trading-day default for the settlement slider
- `finalElapsed(settleDoy)` / `cashFlowSchedule(settleDoy)` — Chapter 3, builds the illustrative (annual-coupon) bond's schedule with PV
- `modifiedDuration(flows, yld)` — Chapter 3, PV-weighted duration feeding the bps yield-impact stat
- `drawTimeline(settleDoy, flows)` — Chapter 3 (called by `update()` on every slider input or button change)
- `showPage(n)` / `PAGES` — book navigation (see Book Structure above); replaced the old `switchTab(n)` / 3-button tab bar

### Module script note
The file is `<script type="module">` (needed to `import` from `shared/src/settlement.js` and `shared/src/csv.js`). Module-scope functions are not global. The old tab bar used inline `onclick="switchTab(n)"` and needed a `window.switchTab = switchTab` bridge; the new TOC/pager avoids that entirely — every control (`#tocBtn`, `#prevBtn`, `#nextBtn`, TOC `<li>` entries) is wired via `addEventListener` in the module script, so no `window.*` exports are needed for navigation.
