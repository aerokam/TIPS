TIPS Ladder Manager — Help

A free, open-source contribution to the Bogleheads community. All calculations run locally in your browser. Prices updated daily ~1 PM ET from FedInvest (mid-market reference).

Rebalance — for existing ladder holders rebalancing into newly issued TIPS. Assumes you want to keep using the same lower bracket TIPS you originally purchased.
Build — designs a new ladder from scratch with zero existing holdings.

Key Concepts

DARA — Desired Annual Real Amount. Your target annual amount in today's (inflation-adjusted) dollars. Each rung is sized to produce this amount.

Gap Years — Years with no issued TIPS (currently 2037–2039). The ladder holds additional TIPS in bracket years flanking the gap to match the average duration of the missing maturities.

Future 30Y Rungs — Years beyond the currently issued TIPS (e.g. 2057–2066). Like gaps, these are covered by duration-matched excess in existing long-dated bonds (currently the 2052/2056 pair).

Bracket Years — Maturity years used to cover gaps or Future 30Y rungs. Upper bracket: always Feb 2040. Lower bracket: usually the latest 10-year TIPS before the gap. In 3-bracket mode, your original lower bracket is preserved, and a second "new lower" is bought to supplement.

Role Playing (LMI) — When bracket or cover TIPS of a given maturity substitute for TIPS that haven't yet been issued (gap years or Future 30Y rungs), they "role play" by contributing their annual interest to the funded year amount for that maturity. Example: If excess Feb 2056s are held to cover Future 30Y rungs (e.g., 2057–2066), the interest from those excess 2056 TIPS contributes to the 2056 funded year amount, which could reduce the quantity of 2056 TIPS required for the 2056 funded year.

Ref CPI — Reference CPI value used for all inflation adjustments. Defaults to the settlement date. Click the Ref CPI value in the info strip to override the reference date (useful for projecting with a future or past CPI). Changing it clears any auto-inferred DARA.

Traceability & Deep Drill

Every number in the UI is explainable. Click any underlined value (like Amount or Cost) to open a Level 2 drill-down showing the calculation chain. Inside those popups, you can click variables like Interest from longer-dated bonds or Ref CPI for a Level 3 breakdown of the underlying math and Level 4 deep-links to the official Code of Federal Regulations.

Mode: Rebalance

For existing TIPS ladder holders. Sells excess bracket TIPS and buys into newly available maturities (former gap years). Support for extending the ladder beyond 2040 is included.

Last Year — Automatically detected from holdings. If your last maturity is 2040, it defaults to 2040 (or the last gap year, 2039). If your holdings end before 2040, that year is used. You can override this to extend the ladder to 2041+; the tool will identify the appropriate TIPS for those years automatically.

Brackets — 2-bracket: always uses the latest Jan 10-year TIPS before the gap (Jan 2036) as the lower bracket; any older lower bracket holdings (e.g. Jan 2034) become regular funded years and their full excess is sold. 3-bracket (default): lower bracket excess is managed with a priority rule — when over-allocated, Jan 2034 excess is sold only as far as restoring the duration match requires (not depleted outright unless the match still needs more), and Jan 2036 excess only if Jan 2034 is fully sold and still over target; when under-allocated, Jan 2034 is frozen at current and Jan 2036 is bought to fill the gap. Jan 2034 excess is never increased or bought back. (A very small residual trade, at most about one bond, can appear on a reload after a partial sell — an ordinary whole-bond-rounding artifact, not an error.) Click the ? next to the Brackets selector for a quick comparison.

DARA — Target annual real amount. Auto-computed from holdings on file load (equilibrium value that makes the rebalance self-financing). After loading, a per-year table lets you override individual year targets — changes may result in non-zero net cash. The DARA field shows by year when any year's target differs from the default.

Two-Segment DARA (LMP / Speculative) — At the top of the per-year table you can pick a split year to treat one holdings file as two ladders: the LMP (liability-matching portfolio, years ≤ split, intended to spend down) and speculative holdings (years > split, e.g. for heirs). Choosing a split year only sets the boundary — nothing is inferred automatically; you decide what to infer. Infer LMP DARA and Infer speculative DARA each solve one segment independently and never rewrite the other segment's per-year DARA. The speculative TIPS' income still funds the LMP — but as the natural coupon flow (LMI+AMD) at rebalance time, not by changing LMP targets. Because the LMP solves against the current speculative DARA, infer/finalize speculative first and the LMP last for whole-portfolio net cash ≈ 0. Type a constant in a segment's $ each field (Enter/blur to apply) to override; individual rungs stay editable. Every bulk change is undoable — Undo (Ctrl+Z) or Revert to loaded restores prior values without reloading the CSV. Click the ? next to Split year for details.

Net Cash — Net proceeds (sells minus buys). Negative = net purchase.

Mode: Build

Designs a new TIPS ladder from scratch with zero existing holdings.

DARA (required) — Enter your target annual real amount. After entering a value, a per-year table appears where you can override the target for individual funded years. The DARA field shows by year when any year's target differs from the default.

First Year / Last Year — Range of funded years. First year defaults to the current calendar year. The per-year DARA table updates automatically when you change the range, preserving any custom values already entered.

Maturity pick — which TIPS fill each funded year. The DARA target stays annual; when more than one TIPS is chosen for a year, the year's amount is split evenly across them (latest maturity absorbs the rounding). Last: the latest-maturing TIPS (default, one per year). First: the earliest-maturing. Semiannual: the January and July TIPS (out to ~2035; single TIPS beyond). All maturity months: one TIPS per distinct maturity month (quarterly out to ~2030). When a month has two issues, the higher-coupon TIPS is used.

Pre-ladder interest — If checked, coupon payments received before the ladder starts (during the years between today and the first funded year) is applied to reduce the quantity needed for the earliest rungs.

Total Cost — full ladder purchase cost.

Duration Matching

Synthetic TIPS are created for gap years using yield interpolation between the bracket anchors. Additional TIPS are split between brackets so their weighted duration matches the average duration of the gap years:

2-bracket:  lowerWeight × lowerDur + upperWeight × upperDur = gapAvgDur
3-bracket:  w1 (orig lower, fixed) + w2 (new lower) + w3 (upper) = 1
            w1×d1 + w2×d2 + w3×d3 = gapAvgDur  →  unique solution given w1

Methodology & Authority

All calculations follow official U.S. Treasury formulas. Reference CPI values are calculated using daily interpolation per 31 CFR § 356 Appendix B. The Ref CPI for the 1st of any month is the CPI-U (NSA) from three months prior. All principal and interest amounts are shown in real terms (today's dollars).
