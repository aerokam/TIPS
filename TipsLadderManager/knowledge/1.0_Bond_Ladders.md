## Bond Ladder Concepts

A [Bond Ladder](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#bond-ladder) has one rung per time interval. **In this specification each
rung is one calendar year**; monthly and quarterly constructions are possible and are not covered
here.

Terms: [Funded Year](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#funded-year) and its synonym [Rung](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#rung),
[Ladder](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ladder), [DAA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#daa), [AA](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#aa),
[LMI](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#lmi), [Last-Year Interest](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#last-year-interest), and
[P+I](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#pi).

## Construction Method

**Work backwards from longest to shortest maturity**

**Why Work Backwards:**
- Final funded year has no later maturities \u2014 simple calculation
- Each earlier year requires interest from later maturities in its calculation
- Processing longest-to-shortest ensures later year values are computed before earlier years reference them

---

## Basic Algorithm

1. Sort securities by maturity (longest first)
2. For each security (longest to shortest):
   - Calculate annual interest contribution
   - Store by maturity year for use by earlier funded years
3. For each funded year:
   - B = Set of all securities maturing in that year (can include multiple maturity months)
   - ILM = Sum(interest from later maturities)
   - Required (P+I) = DAA - ILM
   - Quantity = ROUND(Required (P+I) / (P+I per security))
   - AA = Quantity * (P+I per security) + ILM

**Where:**
- B = Securities maturing in funded year
- ILM = Interest from Later Maturities
- P+I = Principal + Last-Year Interest
- DAA = Desired Annual Amount
- AA = Annual Amount (actual)
