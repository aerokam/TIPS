# 1.0 Seasonal Adjustments (SA)

## Overview
Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS) yields are calculated based on the "**[Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price)**", which is Canty's term for the unadjusted price; this price does not include the inflation adjustment component. However, since TIPS are indexed to the Non-Seasonally Adjusted CPI (CPI-NSA), their monthly performance is subject to predictable seasonal patterns (e.g., higher inflation in spring, lower in autumn).

The **[Seasonal Adjustment (SA)](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor)** adjusts these yields by applying SA factors, providing a more accurate comparison of real yields across different months of the year by adjusting the yields to be consistent with institutional traders' views of seasonal impacts on nominal returns.

---

## Explaining Seasonality

### Level 1: Common Sense
Prices for things like gas, clothing, and food go up and down with the seasons (e.g., higher demand for gas in summer). This "noise" makes it look like inflation is moving faster or slower than it really is. We "fix" the math by smoothing out these seasonal waves so you can see the real value of the TIPS without being fooled by the time of year.

### Level 2: The Boglehead Perspective
Evidence-based investing requires accurate data. A raw TIPS yield in April might look higher than one in October simply because of historical inflation patterns, not because the bond is actually a better deal. Seasonal adjustment allows for a "fair" comparison across the entire yield curve by removing predictable, recurring CPI-NSA fluctuations.

### Level 3: Institutional Logic
Institutional TIPS traders (the ones who set the market prices) don't trade based on raw NSA yields. They look at "Seasonally Adjusted" yields to identify "cheap" or "rich" spots on the curve. By applying these same factors, we are viewing the market through the same lens as the professionals.

### Level 4: The Math
We apply multiplicative seasonal factors derived from the BLS CPI-U series (Comparing the Non-Seasonally Adjusted index to the Seasonally Adjusted index).
- **Formula**: $SA\_Factor = \frac{Index_{NSA}}{Index_{SA}}$
- **Yield Impact**: We adjust the TIPS price by the ratio of the settlement factor to the maturity factor, then re-calculate the Internal Rate of Return (IRR).

---

## The Transformation

The seasonal adjustment is Canty (2009) Equation 14, the working approximation the apps use ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §4):

\[ SACP \approx CP \times \frac{S_{Settle}}{S_{Maturity}} \]

### 1. In TIPS terms

Canty writes for inflation-linked bonds in general. For TIPS the notation maps as follows ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §1a has the full table):

| Canty | TIPS term | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| \( CP \) | [Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#clean-price) | the quoted real price, before inflation adjustment |
| \( SACP \) | [Seasonally Adjusted Clean Price](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sacp) | the clean price with predictable seasonal carry removed |
| \( S_t \) | [SA Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-factor) on date \( t \) | \( RefCPI_{NSA}(t) / RefCPI_{SA}(t) \), the seasonal component of the Ref CPI |
| \( S_{Settle} / S_{Maturity} \) | [SA Price Factor](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-price-factor) | the multiplier applied to the clean price |

In app terms the equation is `SACP ≈ CP × SA_Price_Factor`, where `SA_Price_Factor = SA_Factor(settlement date) / SA_Factor(maturity date)`.

`CP` is whichever quoted price is in use; the formula does not depend on the choice (see [Data Sources](#data-sources)).

The **[SA Yield](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#sa-yield)** is the ordinary yield-to-maturity of `SACP` (Actual/Actual day count, semi-annual compounding). Seasonally adjusted breakeven inflation is the nominal yield minus the SA Yield.

When settlement and maturity fall on the same calendar month and day (a whole number of years apart), the two SA Factors are equal, the SA Price Factor is 1.0, and `SACP = CP`, so there is no adjustment. The SA Price Factor departs from 1.0 only by the seasonal difference over the leftover partial year ([Canty.md](Canty.md) §4, the anniversary identity).

### 2. Getting the two SA Factors

Both SA Factors are read from `RefCpiNsaSa.csv` through `shared/src/ref-cpi.js#saFactorForDate`.

- **Settlement date**: a recent date, inside the published series, so its SA Factor is the exact daily value for that date.
- **Maturity date**: normally years past the end of the series, which extends only about three months beyond the latest CPI release. Its SA Factor is the value for the most recent earlier date with the same month and day.

The maturity lookup relies on the assumption Canty's model is built on: the seasonal pattern is periodic, recurrent, and constant from one year to the next (\( I_t = T_t\,S_t \) with \( S_t \) fixed by calendar position). If that held exactly, the most recent year's SA Factor for the maturity month and day would be the correct value. A multi-year average of that calendar day was considered, in case the latest year is not representative of the next, but testing showed it changes the maturity SA Factor too little to be worth the added complexity.

## Data Sources
- **Prices**: Sourced from FedInvest (daily) or uploaded via Broker CSV (Schwab/Fidelity).
  - **Broker**: Provides **Ask Prices** (actionable buy prices).
  - **FedInvest**: Provides **Mid-Market Prices** (midpoint of bid and ask). Because mid-market prices are lower than ask prices, FedInvest yields will appear higher than broker ask yields.
- **SA Factors**: `RefCpiNsaSa.csv` in R2, one row per calendar day holding `Ref CPI NSA`, `Ref CPI SA`, and their ratio (the SA Factor).
  - `Ref CPI SA` applies the 31 CFR Appendix B daily interpolation, the same interpolation used for the Ref CPI, to BLS's monthly seasonally adjusted CPI-U.
  - BLS publishes the seasonally adjusted CPI-U only monthly, and Appendix B officially interpolates the non-seasonally adjusted series alone. A daily seasonally adjusted Ref CPI has no official form: it is a constructed series, built for seasonal yield comparison, always calculated and never retrieved because there is nothing official to retrieve. See [DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi](../../knowledge/DATA_DICTIONARY.md#ref-cpi).
  - The interpolation is defined once in `shared/src/ref-cpi.js` and imported wherever it is needed.
