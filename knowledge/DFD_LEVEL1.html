Level 1

Process 1 covers every acquisition process. It explodes at Level 2 into the individual jobs, one per store it writes. All fourteen R2 stores are drawn here, whether one app reads a store or several. External entities are not redrawn at this level; their twelve flows are shown against each entity on the context diagram and enter here as one flow. Every app carries the same pair of flows to the user, labelled once at the top. The user is drawn once, as a tall shape, so no flow to it crosses another.
