Level 1

Process 1 covers every acquisition process the apps share. The processes and stores used by one app alone are drawn inside that app at Level 2, not here. The seven stores shown are the ones more than one process reads. External entities are not redrawn at this level; their twelve flows are shown against each entity on the context diagram, and enter here as one flow. Every app carries the same pair of flows to the user, labelled once at the top.
