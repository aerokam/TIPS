Level 2 — Yield Curves

No process here writes a data store. Yield Curves reads five stores and computes everything else in the browser at load, including the spot curves, which flow to 7.7 and no further. To take a fitted spot curve into a spreadsheet, it would first have to be written somewhere. 7.1 is the only process that reads a store; the rest take their input from each other. GSW curve parameters are the Federal Reserve’s own published fit, read as a reference line rather than computed here.
