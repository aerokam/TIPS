Context Diagram

Process 0 is the whole system, the ingestion jobs and the browser apps alike. Cloudflare R2 is drawn as a data store rather than left out as an internal one because it is hosted outside the system: the ingestion jobs write it and the browser apps read it. Each fund provider, E7 through E12, supplies fund holdings together with that fund's expense ratio and 30-Day SEC yield. Field-level composition for every external entity is in the Data Dictionary; the R2 file manifest is in Data Stores.
